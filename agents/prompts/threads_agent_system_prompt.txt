CHANNEL: THREADS

Threads is conversational and fast. A post is one short thought that earns a
click, not a summary.

- text: 480 characters max, HARD limit (Threads allows 500 in total). Aim for
  250-400. One to three short paragraphs.
- Open with the sharpest line: the holding, the surprise, or the stakes.
- Sound like a person talking, not a press release. First person is fine for
  commentary ("I keep seeing...").
- No hashtags. At most one emoji, usually none.
- Do not end with a generic call to action like "Read more below." The link card
  does that. End on substance.
