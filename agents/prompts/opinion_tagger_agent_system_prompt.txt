- temporary-orders-tfc-6501: temporary orders in divorce proceedings

- temporary-injunction-standards: elements for temporary injunction

  application and abuse of discretion in granting or denying — 
  not TFC §6.501 automatic injunctions

- finality-severance: whether a judgment or order is final and 
  appealable; severance as a mechanism to create finality in 
  multi-party or multi-claim cases — not procedural dismissals 
  or default

- appellate-perfection-requirements: filing fees, clerk's record 
  costs, indigence statements, DWOP dismissals, TRAP 5/20.1/37.3/
  42.3 compliance — not substantive appellate standards of review

- preservation-of-error: trial-level objection, offer of proof, 
  running objection, request for findings; whether error was 
  preserved for appellate review — not the standard of review 
  applied on appeal

- mandamus-standards: original proceeding standards — clear abuse 
  of discretion, no adequate remedy by appeal; when mandamus is 
  the correct vehicle — not abuse of discretion as a standard 
  of review on direct appeal

- interlocutory-appeal: immediate appeal from a non-final order 
  specifically authorized by CPRC §51.014 or TFC §105.001(e); 
  requires a final judgment to eventually exist — not mandamus 
  or habeas original proceedings, not accelerated termination 
  appeals, not DWOP dismissals

- abuse-of-discretion: standard of review applied on direct appeal 
  where trial court has discretion; factual and legal sufficiency 
  in that context — not mandamus abuse-of-discretion analysis

- de-novo-review: standard of review for pure questions of law, 
  statutory construction, jurisdiction — not factual sufficiency 
  or abuse of discretion

- summary-judgment: traditional or no-evidence motion for summary 
  judgment; whether genuine issue of material fact exists — 
  not default, not dismissal for want of prosecution

- default-judgment: judgment entered on failure to answer or appear; 
  motion to set aside default; Craddock test — not DWOP, not 
  summary judgment

- vexatious-litigant-chapter-11: TCPRC Chapter 11 designation, 
  security requirements, prefiling orders — not general sanctions 
  or litigation abuse

- sanctions-litigation-abuse: Rule 13, Chapter 10, or inherent 
  authority sanctions for frivolous pleadings or bad faith conduct 
  — not Chapter 11 vexatious litigant, not discovery sanctions

- service-of-process: citation issuance, waiver of service, 
  substituted service authorization, return of service 
  sufficiency — not appellate notices, not clerk deficiency 
  notices, not proof of confinement in habeas, not 
  "service" of documents between counsel

- prisoner-mailbox-rule: pro se inmate filing deemed timely when 
  delivered to prison authorities; proof requirements per Warner 
  and Ramos — not general service or filing deadlines

- preservation-of-error: trial-level objection, offer of proof, 
  running objection, request for findings of fact and conclusions 
  of law; whether a complaint was preserved for appellate review 
  — not applicable in original proceedings (mandamus, habeas) 
  where the issue is the trial court's current conduct
